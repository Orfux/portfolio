## Idea

Maintenance teams searched hundreds of pages of PDF manuals for fault codes. The assistant understands the question, finds the right page and answers **with its source**.

## How it works

1. Manuals are split into chunks and stored in a vector database.
2. For each question the most relevant chunks are retrieved (RAG).
3. The LLM answers only from those chunks and cites page numbers.
