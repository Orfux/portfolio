## Problem

Three production lines were monitored from separate panels. Faults were noticed late and production data was copied into Excel by hand.

## Solution

- All PLCs were connected to a single SCADA server over **OPC UA**.
- Overview screens per line and detailed machine screens were designed for operators.
- Alarm handling was prioritised; critical alarms are sent to the shift supervisor by SMS.
- Production and downtime data is written to SQL and daily reports are generated automatically.

## Result

| Metric | Before | After |
|---|---|---|
| Response time to faults | ~25 min | ~8 min |
| Unplanned downtime | — | 35% lower |
| Reporting | Manual | Automatic |
