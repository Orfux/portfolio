## About the project

For the TEKNOFEST Model Satellite Competition, our team built a model satellite that collects data during its descent and sends it to a ground station. From January to August 2025 I worked both as **team captain** and as the **engineer responsible for energy and power distribution**.

## Team captain

- Coordinated technical tasks across a 5+ person engineering team.
- Managed the project timeline so that mechanical, electronics and software work could progress without blocking each other.

## Power distribution and energy management

On a small satellite every milliamp counts. Sensors, the radio module and the flight computer run at different voltage levels, and current spikes can make the supply unstable.

- Designed the **power distribution and energy management architecture** for all on-board components.
- Ensured **stable voltage regulation** across every component.

## System integration

Subsystems that work on their own can still fail once combined. Through integration and troubleshooting work we **reduced the hardware failure rate by 30%**.

## What I learned

Beyond the technical side, this project taught me how to steer a team toward one goal, set priorities and think about problems at the system level. It is also where my interest in energy management started — the root of my interest in EMS, BESS and solar systems today.
